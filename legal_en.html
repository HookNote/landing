2. What HookNote Does

HookNote performs only two functions:

  1. Record audio and send it to user-configured webhooks.
  2. Send text written by the user to those webhooks.

The app is free, ad-free, and does not include analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Processed Data

HookNote only processes the minimum data necessary to function:

  • Content you enter: audio or text.
  • Configured webhooks: alias and URL (1 to 5).
  • Local metadata: delivery status, date/time.

ℹ️ No data is collected, stored, or processed on the developer's servers.

4. Purpose and Legal Basis (GDPR)

5. Where and How Data is Processed
